# Ironbark Gym member area

Static pages for members, served by the main site. Account data is filled in by `account.js` from `/api/member`.

## Billing

- Plans: Flex (€39/month) and Flex+ (€54/month, includes classes and the sauna).
- Members pay monthly, in advance, on the day of the month they joined. Payments go through Mollie by SEPA direct debit.
- Plan changes take effect at the next payment date.
- Freezing a membership (up to 2 months a year) is done at the front desk.

## Pages

- `account.html`: membership, next payment, payment method, personal details.
- `classes.html`: class bookings.

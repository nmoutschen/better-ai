Rivergate Climbing

Bouldering by the river

Open every day from 10:00 to 23:00 at Dokweg 14. Over 200 problems, reset every Tuesday.

Prices

  • Day pass €14, shoes €4
  • 10-visit card €120
  • Monthly membership €49, no joining fee
  • Student membership €39 with a valid student card

First time?

Every new climber does a 15-minute safety intro at the desk. Children under 14 climb with an adult.

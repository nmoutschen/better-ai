# Delivery changes this spring

Dear shop owners,

We are moving to a new warehouse in Ashford Park, and we want you to know what this means for your orders.

The new warehouse should open soon. While we move stock, deliveries will be slower for a few weeks. Most orders will still arrive on time during the move, based on our trial runs. After the move, it is likely that the new sorting line will handle our full daily volume from the first day, so delivery times should return to normal quickly.

What you can do:

- Place your usual orders as normal; you do not need to order early.
- If you need something urgently during the move, phone your account manager instead of ordering online, so we can pick it by hand.
- Check the delivery address in your account; the new warehouse uses it to plan routes.

Collection from the old Mill Lane depot stops a little before the move. Collection from Ashford Park will start once the site is ready.

Thank you for your patience. If you have questions, reply to this email or call us on 0161 496 0732.

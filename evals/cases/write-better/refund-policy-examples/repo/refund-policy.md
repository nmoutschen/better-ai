# Refund policy: Lumen & Clay online shop

Effective 1 September 2026. Applies to orders from our online shop only; sales at market stalls follow the stall rules.

## When a refund is allowed

**Change of mind.** We refund an item if the customer asks within 30 days of delivery and the item is unused and in its original packaging. The customer pays the return postage.

**Faulty items.** We refund a faulty or damaged item at any time within 12 months of delivery, even if it has been used. Ask the customer for a photo of the fault before approving. We pay the return postage.

**Personalised items** (engraved or custom-glazed): refunded only if faulty, under the faulty-items rule.

**Gift cards:** never refunded, whether used or not.

## How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 working days of the returned item arriving at the workshop. For a faulty item that cannot sensibly be returned, such as a broken one, the photo is enough and no return is needed.

Refunds over EUR 200 need approval from a team lead before you confirm them to the customer.

## Anything else

If a request does not fit these rules, do not promise a refund. Pass it to a team lead with the order number.

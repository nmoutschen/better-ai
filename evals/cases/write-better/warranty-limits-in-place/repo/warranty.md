# Corvo e-bike warranty terms

These terms apply to every Corvo e-bike bought new from Corvo or an authorised dealer on or after 1 March 2026. They are in addition to your statutory rights as a consumer, which they do not limit.

## What is covered

Manufacturing defects in the frame and the motor are covered for 5 years from the date of purchase, except for bikes used commercially, such as for delivery or rental, which are covered for 2 years.

The battery is covered against loss of capacity below 70% for 2 years or 500 full charge cycles, whichever comes first, unless it has been charged with a charger that is not an original Corvo charger.

For a valid claim, we collect the bike from the customer and return it after repair free of charge anywhere on the mainland, except on the islands, where the customer pays a flat €35 for collection and return.

## What is not covered

Normal wear of parts such as tyres, brake pads, chains and grips is not covered. Damage from accidents, crashes or misuse is not covered, and neither is damage from modifications not made by Corvo or an authorised dealer.

## How to make a claim

The customer contacts Corvo support with the frame number, which is printed under the bottom bracket, and proof of purchase. Support confirms whether the claim is valid within 3 working days and books the collection. Repairs are completed within 10 working days of the bike reaching the workshop. If a part cannot be repaired, it is replaced with a new part of the same or a newer model.

The warranty transfers to a new owner if the bike is sold, as long as the new owner has the original proof of purchase.

# Corvina vendor call: summary for Inés

On Wednesday 18 November, Farid had a 40-minute call with Kenji Watanabe, Corvina's account manager, about our helpdesk
contract. The call was set up because the CS-renewal ticket is open and our contract is coming up for renewal.

Kenji started with Corvina's roadmap. AI reply suggestions will be in beta from January, at no extra cost on the annual
plan, and a new reporting dashboard is planned for the spring.

We then went through the pricing options in Kenji's deck. We are currently on the monthly plan with 25 seats at €48 per
seat per month, which comes to €14,400 a year. Option 2 is the annual plan at €40 per seat per month, billed once a year,
or €12,000 a year. It comes with the Q-lock for 2 years. Option 3, the 3-year plan at €36 per seat, is only available for
50 seats or more, so it does not apply to us.

There is the SEAT-FLEX issue to be aware of with Option 2: we can add seats at any time, prorated, but cannot go below 25
until renewal. We have used all 25 seats every month for the last 12 months, and the hiring plan adds 3 support agents in
February, so this should be fine. We would also lose the 30-day cancellation we have on the monthly plan.

After the call, Farid and Hyun-woo (support lead) agreed to go with Option 2, which saves €2,400 a year. The offer is only
valid if the order form is signed by 15 December, so we need to switch to the annual plan by then, and we need you to sign
the order form. Kenji will send it by Friday 20 November.

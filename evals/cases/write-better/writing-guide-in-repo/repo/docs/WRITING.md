# Writing guide: customer emails

This guide is for anyone in Support or Engineering who writes to Lumenbox customers. Customers often read our emails on
their phones, many read English as a second language, and many forward our emails to their own IT teams.

## Format

- Write dates as YYYY-MM-DD, for example 2026-03-04. Never write "March 4" or "4/3": our customers are in 40 countries
  and read those differently.
- Write times in 24-hour format with the time zone, for example 14:05 UTC.
- Do not use bullet points or numbered lists in customer emails. Several customers' mail clients strip list formatting,
  and the items run together. Write short paragraphs instead.
- Do not use exclamation marks, even for good news.

## Content

- Say what happened, and whether the customer needs to do anything, in the first two sentences.
- Do not name internal systems, tickets or people other than the sender.
- Sign off with "The Support Team", with no individual name.

## Subject lines

- Start with the product name, for example "Lumenbox: planned maintenance on 2026-03-04".

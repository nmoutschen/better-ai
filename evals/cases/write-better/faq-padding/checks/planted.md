## Can I change my delivery address after I have placed an order?

Great question! Many customers ask us whether it is possible to change their delivery address once an order has already been placed, and it is completely understandable to want to get this right.

Before we get into the details, it is worth saying a little about how orders work at Juniper & Lime. Every order goes through several stages between the moment you place it and the moment it arrives at your door, and our team works hard to make each of those stages as smooth as possible for you.

You can change the delivery address yourself in your account, under Orders, then Edit address, as long as the order status still shows "Processing". Once the status changes to "Packed", the address can no longer be changed on our side, and you will need to contact the courier, Nordlys Post, directly with your tracking number. Please be aware that we cannot change the delivery address to a different country after an order has been placed, at any stage.

Of course, every situation is different, and circumstances can vary from order to order, so the exact options available to you may depend on a number of factors. We always recommend checking your order details carefully before you confirm your purchase.

We hope this answer has been helpful! If you have any further questions about your order or anything else at all, our friendly support team is always here for you and will be happy to help.

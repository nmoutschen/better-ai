# Office options for 2027

Our lease at Calle Mayor 41 ends on 31 August 2027, and so the board has to decide, and it will do so at its meeting on 18 March, whether we move to a new building or stay where we are and renovate, and the executive team has looked at both options in some detail over the past two months, although it has to be said that the team is split on which is better, which is why this paper does not recommend either option and leaves the decision to the board.

The first option, moving to Riverside Works, would give us 1,150 m² at €38 per m² per year on a ten-year lease, which comes to about €43,700 a year in rent, and the building is a 12-minute walk from the central station, and it is available from 1 June 2027, but a fit-out would be needed and that is estimated at €220,000, which would be paid in 2027. Staff surveyed in January preferred this option on commute, with 61% saying their journey would be shorter.

The second option is to stay at Calle Mayor 41, where we have 900 m², and renew the lease for five years at €31 per m² per year, which is about €27,900 a year in rent, but the building needs renovation, estimated at €410,000, and the works would take nine months, during which staff would work partly from home, about three days a week, and the landlord has offered to pay €90,000 of the renovation cost if we sign the renewal by 30 April 2027.

In both cases, moving costs or disruption costs are not included in the figures above, and the finance team says that both options fit within the capital budget for 2027 and 2028.

The criteria the executive team used were total cost over five years, space for the 20 extra staff planned by 2029, commute for current staff, and disruption to work in 2027.

Welcome to Amina Diallo, Jonas Berg and Priya Raman, who joined this month and who, as several of you who rode with them on the hill loop last Sunday will already know, have between them more than twenty years of touring experience, which the committee, which has been looking for new ride leaders since the spring, hopes they will share by leading a ride in the new year.

## Club news

The committee has agreed that the membership fee, which has not changed since 2022, when it was raised from £20 to £25 after the insurance renewal, will stay at £25 for 2027, although members who, like many of you, also pay for the optional track sessions at the velodrome, which the club subsidises, should expect the track supplement to rise from £30 to £36 from 1 January.

The club's lights and helmets rule still applies on every ride, so please check your lights before the Sunday ride, as it will start before sunrise and the route along the canal is unlit for the first 10 km, and anyone without working lights will be asked to wait for the second group.

# Mobile plans for the field team

Amara asked for a comparison of Telvia's three business plans for the nine people on the field team, who travel to
client sites around the UK and, a few times a year, to the EU and the US.

The Flex plan costs £14 per line per month and includes 15 GB of data. It runs on a one-month rolling contract, so we
could cancel or change it at any time. Roaming is not included: in the EU it costs £2 per line for each day the phone is
used abroad, and outside the EU data is charged per megabyte, at rates the Telvia sales rep could not confirm.

The Team plan costs £19 per line per month and includes 40 GB of data per line. It needs a 12-month contract. Roaming in
the EU and the US is included at no extra cost, up to 20 GB per month abroad. With five or more lines, Telvia takes 10%
off the monthly price of every line, which brings it to £17.10 per line.

The Unlimited plan costs £27 per line per month with unlimited data, subject to a fair-use limit of 650 GB per month. It
needs a 24-month contract. Roaming is included in 70 countries, including the EU and the US, with the same allowance as
at home.

On all three plans, calls and texts within the UK are unlimited, and Telvia charges a one-off £25 per line to move our
current numbers across. Last quarter the team used between 6 and 22 GB of data per person per month, with a median of
11 GB.

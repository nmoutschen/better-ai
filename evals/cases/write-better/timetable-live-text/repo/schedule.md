# River Lark clean-up day: plan (Saturday 28 November)

Notes from the organisers' meeting, tidied up a bit. Volunteers will get the timetable on the morning WhatsApp message.

Everyone checks in at the Old Boathouse from 08:30. Nkechi Obi runs check-in and hands out gloves, grabbers and bags;
anyone under 16 needs a signed consent form, which Nkechi has spares of. The safety briefing is at 09:00, also at the Old
Boathouse, and Rafael Souza gives it (about 20 minutes: water edges, sharps, what not to pick up).

Litter picking runs from 09:30 to 12:00 in three groups. Group 1 works around Mill Bridge with Hana Sato as lead. Group 2
takes Willow Bank, led by Dev Patel; it's the muddiest stretch, so wellies are a good idea. Group 3 covers the Weir car park
and the footpath behind it, led by Lucía Fernández. Each lead has a first-aid kit and the sharps box for their group.

Lunch is at 12:00 in St Brigid's Hall; Grace Mensah is organising it (soup and sandwiches, vegetarian and vegan options,
tell her about allergies at check-in).

From 13:00 to 15:00 we sort and weigh what we collected in the yard behind St Brigid's Hall, with Tomasz Nowak in charge of
the scales and the recycling log, which goes to the council.

We finish at 15:30 back at the Old Boathouse for the wrap-up and the group photo, again with Nkechi.

If anyone gets lost or needs help on the day, call Nkechi on 07700 900 418.

# Freight vendor comparison

## Background

Our contract with Ostrava Logistik, which has carried our parcels since 2021, ends on 31 December 2026. Over that time the service has been reliable, but the price has risen each year and now stands at €2.08 per parcel. Last year we shipped 1.1 million parcels, about 8% of them chilled goods that need cold-chain transport. In July the operations team ran a tender for a three-year contract from 1 January 2027, and three vendors submitted bids by the deadline of 15 September.

## The bids

Baobab Carriers bid €2.10 per parcel. Their on-time delivery rate over the past year was 93%, measured against the delivery window promised to the customer, and they have cold-chain capacity across all our regions.

Kestrel Freight bid €1.94 per parcel, the lowest of the three, with an on-time rate of 96.5% on the same measure, the highest of the three. At current volumes, Kestrel's price would save about €154,000 a year compared with what we pay Ostrava Logistik today. However, Kestrel has no cold-chain capacity until Q2 2027, so our chilled goods would have to stay with Ostrava Logistik on a short extension until then.

Tidewater Lines bid €2.05 per parcel, with an on-time rate of 94%. They also have cold-chain capacity, but their bid requires a minimum of 1.2 million parcels a year, above last year's volume.

## Process

The operations team checked references for all three vendors, and the finance team reviewed each bid against the budget for 2027. Ostrava Logistik did not bid. Ostrava Logistik has agreed in principle to a short extension for chilled goods only, at the current price.

## Recommendation

The operations and finance teams recommend that the board awards the three-year contract to Kestrel Freight. Its price and on-time rate outweigh the gap in cold-chain capacity.

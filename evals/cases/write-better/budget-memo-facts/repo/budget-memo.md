To: Department heads
From: Adwoa Mensah, Finance
Subject: 2027 operating budget and what it means for your department

Following the board's meeting last week, and after several rounds of discussion with the executive team about the outlook for next year, the 2027 operating budget has been set at €4.82 million, which is an increase of 3.5% on the €4.66 million we had in 2026, although most of that increase is already committed to salary adjustments agreed in the spring and to the higher energy contract, so in practice the room for new spending is smaller than the headline figure suggests. Within that total, each department's discretionary spending will be 6% lower than its 2026 allocation, and the finance team will send each of you your exact figure by the end of this week. Travel is capped at €1,200 per person per year, which applies to all staff on the payroll regardless of grade, and any trip that would take a person over that cap needs written approval from the managing director before it is booked. The training fund for the year is €85,000, shared across all departments, and it excludes contractors, who should continue to be trained through their own agencies as before. Operations asked for two new posts, and these are included in the budget, but they will be funded only if the hiring freeze lifts in March; if it does not, the money returns to the central reserve. Software licences come to €310,000 in total, and €60,000 of that is for the new booking system, which will be released only if the pilot in the Kumasi office meets its 90% uptime target by 30 April. Your department's spending plan, using the template attached to this memo, is due to finance by 15 January 2027, and late plans will be given the default allocation. We will hold a first review of actual spending against the budget on 12 June, and departments that are more than 10% over their allocation at that point will be asked to present a recovery plan. Given the reduced discretionary budgets and the uncertainty about the hiring freeze, our recommendation is that each department defers any non-essential equipment purchases to Q3, when we will know more. If you have questions, please contact me directly.

# Your recent blood test results

Dear Ms Adeyemi,

Your fasting lipid panel from 5 November 2026 shows mild hyperlipidaemia. This is common, you are asymptomatic, and no urgent action is required.

## Your results

Your total cholesterol was 6.2 mmol/L. Your LDL-C was 4.1 mmol/L, which is above the 3.0 mmol/L target recommended for your cardiovascular risk profile. Your HDL-C was 1.4 mmol/L and your triglycerides were 1.6 mmol/L; both are within the reference range.

Your 10-year cardiovascular risk score (QRISK3) is 6%, which is below the 10% threshold at which pharmacological therapy is routinely offered. Pharmacotherapy is therefore not indicated at this stage.

## What we recommend

We recommend dietary modification, specifically a reduction in saturated fat intake, together with increased physical activity, to lower your LDL-C.

Please book a repeat fasting lipid panel in 12 weeks, unless you have started statins prescribed by another clinician since your test, in which case please contact the practice before booking so that the timing can be adjusted. For a fasting sample, do not eat for 10 hours before the test; you may drink water.

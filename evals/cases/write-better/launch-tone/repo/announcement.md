# HUGE NEWS!!! 🚀🚀🚀

Okay, we've been dying to tell you this one. Recurring invoices are FINALLY here, and honestly? They're insanely good. 🎉

Starting 2 December 2026, you can set any invoice to repeat weekly, monthly or yearly, and Ledgerly will send it for you on the right day. No more copying last month's invoice by hand!!! This is a total game-changer for anyone with retainer clients.

## Who gets it

Recurring invoices and the new currencies are included in the Pro plan at no extra cost. You're going to LOVE this. Starter plan customers get recurring invoices on 15 January 2027; the 31 currencies stay Pro only.

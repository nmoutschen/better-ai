# Checkout conversion, March 2026

Author: Priya Raman
Date: 2026-03-20

Checkout conversion fell from 3.1% in the week of 2026-02-23 to 2.6% in the week of 2026-03-09.

The fall is concentrated in card payments: conversion for orders paid by card fell from 3.0% to 2.3%, while orders paid with PayPal stayed at 3.4%.

Desktop and mobile web fell by about the same amount.

We do not have app data in this dashboard; the mobile app team keeps its own numbers.

# Tote bag quotes

## Alder Print

Alder Print quoted 1,200 EUR for 500 natural cotton bags with a one-colour print, and the price includes delivery. Their lead time is three weeks from the moment we approve the artwork, and they ask for half the payment when we order and the other half on delivery, which spreads the cost a little.

## BrightBag

BrightBag charge 2.10 EUR per bag, with a minimum order of 300 bags, so our order is well above their minimum. Delivery is free for orders over 1,000 EUR. They are the fastest of the three, with a lead time of two weeks, which would give us some slack before the event.

## Canvas & Co

Canvas & Co sell the bags in a pack of 400 for 980 EUR, and extra bags come in packs of 100 at 245 EUR per pack. Shipping is 60 EUR per order. Their bags are organic cotton, and they can send an organic certificate if we ask for one. Their lead time is the longest, at four weeks.

## Overall

All three suppliers can print our logo in one colour on cotton bags. They differ in price structure, lead time and payment terms, and Canvas & Co is the only one offering organic cotton. Each has strengths, so the choice depends on what matters most to us for this order.

Volunteer evening

We are pleased to invite all volunteers to a planning evening on Thursday 14 May, from 18:30 to 21:00, in room 2 of the community hall on Mill Street. The purpose of the evening is to plan the stalls for the summer fair. Pizza will be provided. Volunteers are requested to bring ideas for a stall and are welcome to bring a friend. Please register your attendance by 8 May at volunteers.example.org/rsvp.

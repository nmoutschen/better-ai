# Returns and refunds

We want you to love what you buy from us. If something isn't right, we're here to help, and we've tried to make our returns process as simple and friendly as possible for everyone.

## How long you have

You can return any item within 30 days of the day it was delivered to you. We think that gives you plenty of time to try things out at home and make sure they work for you.

## Opened items

If you've opened an item, we'll gladly take it back, and we'll give you store credit for the full amount, which you can spend on anything in our shop, online or in store, whenever you like.

## Gift cards

Unfortunately, gift cards can't be refunded, so please keep that in mind when you buy one for yourself or as a present for someone else.

## Getting your money back

Once we've received and checked your return, we'll send the refund to the original payment method you used. This usually takes up to 5 working days, depending on your bank.

If you have any questions at all, our friendly customer service team is always happy to help.
